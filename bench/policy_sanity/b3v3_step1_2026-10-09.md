## What it establishes

- **The canneal gap was the request type, and it is closed.** ChampSim's
  stream of ownership requests to the shared cache now matches gem5's within
  0.3%. Its store-time sharing rate is within 23% (2 MB) and 11% (8 MB),
  high, as ocean's is (26% / 35%).
- **Step 1 changes no simulated behaviour.** Shown three ways:
  1. **On the server under LRU:** all 10 rows are identical in every timing
     and traffic column.
  2. **Locally, five more policies:** COALESCE, srrip_bias, Hawkeye,
     Mockingjay and SHiP on a synthetic 4-core workload of shared RMWs.
     Old and new binaries give identical cycles on every core, and every
     differing line is the load/RFO split or a new counter.
  3. **In the code:**
     - no replacement policy distinguishes an RFO from a LOAD; they
       special-case only WRITE and PREFETCH;
     - the LLC sets MESI state at fill from dirtiness alone
       (`cache.cc:108`);
     - the LLC updates the sharer mask the same way for both.

  **So A5 and every existing policy result stand unchanged by step 1.**
- **barnes is untouched, as predicted.** Its 0.001 is the missing
  invalidation of private copies: step 2.
