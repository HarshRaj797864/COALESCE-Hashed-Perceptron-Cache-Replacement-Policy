## The noise floor

| Class | Runs | s_e | Tie rule δ (log units, df 12) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Fixed 2 MB | 20 (4 policies x 5 windows) | 0.046% | **0.141%** |
| 2 MB a core | 20 | 0.021% | **0.065%** |

Pairs whose sign changes across the five windows:
- fixed: mockingjay / srrip;
- 2 MB a core: srrip_bias / mockingjay and mockingjay / ship.

Every other pair keeps its sign in all 5 windows.

## The readings (fixed)

- **R1, COALESCE's place:**
  - **canneal 8c, fixed 2 MB, shared:** 1st by 0.67% over mockingjay. That
    is above δ 0.141%, with the sign kept in all 5 windows, so **the paper
    may write "COALESCE is 1st".**
  - **canneal 16c, fixed 2 MB, shared:** 1st by 0.06% over srrip. That is
    below δ (no windows at 16c), so **the paper writes "tied for first:
    COALESCE, SRRIP".**
- **R2 and R3: every τ is quoted as τ_b with its exact p.**
  - **P11** (shared against private at one size):

    | Configuration | τ_b | Exact p | Beyond chance? | Noise label |
    |---|---|---|---|---|
    | canneal 4c, fixed 2 MB | +0.778 | 0.0024 | yes | below the noise ceiling |
    | canneal 4c, 2 MB a core | +0.333 | 0.26 | no | below the noise ceiling |

    So **three of P11's four τ may not be called agreement beyond chance**,
    and all four reorderings are beyond window noise.
  - **The size controls:** τ_b from +0.167 to +0.667. Only the two ocean
    controls are beyond chance (+0.611, p 0.025; +0.667, p 0.013). All are
    below the noise ceiling.
  - **P13** (ocean, shared against private):
    - fixed 2 MB: +0.778 (p 0.0024), below the noise ceiling;
    - 2 MB a core: +0.944 (p 5e-5), within noise.
  - **P14** (64 against 16 LLC MSHRs): +1.000 (p 5.5e-6), within noise.
  - **Synchronised against drifting replay:** τ_b +0.722 to +1.000, every
    one beyond chance.
    - Within noise: canneal 4c (both sizes), canneal 8c fixed, canneal 16c
      (both sizes).
    - Below the ceiling: canneal 8c at 2 MB a core, ocean (both sizes).
    - Median +0.889 (bootstrap 95% +0.833 to +1.000).
  - **P12** (canneal, the best gain, shared minus private, in log units):
    - 4c: +0.034;
    - 8c: +0.092.
    - Both are **robust** (the rule needs at least √2 δ = 0.0020).
  - **A6 and C0** are restated with τ_b and exact p, with no tie rule. These
    are drifting traces on the b3v4 binary, which this noise floor does not

- **R4, P11's explanation at 2 MB a core:** both comparisons read below the
