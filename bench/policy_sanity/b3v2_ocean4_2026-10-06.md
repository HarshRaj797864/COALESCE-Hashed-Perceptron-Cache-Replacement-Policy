# B3 v2 on ocean: the sharer directory agrees with gem5 at both LLC sizes

**2026-10-06.** Scored against `b3v2_design_2026-10-05.md`:
- `3a0a085`, with its same-day addendum, both committed before any v2 result
  existed;
- the window method of `b3_design_2026-10-04.md`.

Sources:
- ChampSim: `b3_rows_v2.txt` and `phasea_rows_v11.txt` (368 rows; v10's
  complete rows are byte-identical);
- gem5: `simulator/results/gem5_b3/` (2 MB and 8 MB, stopped at 50 M and
  150 M) and `gem5_ocean_4c_n514/` (whole program, 2 MB).

## Qualifying checks — all pass

- **The three new trees pass `analyze_rows.py check`:** complete, no wrap,
  TOTAL identity, ≥ 100 M instructions per core.
- **The directory changed nothing else.** Every pre-existing column of the
  v2 LRU rows matches:
  - ocean 2 MB and 8 MB, against the `b3_*` rows (5ac2844 binary);
  - barnes 2 MB, against the `phaseA` barnes rows of 2026-09-18 (a much
    older build).
- **Self-check:** 0 mismatches summed over all 12 private caches, and 12
  registered (3 × 4 cores), in every v2 row.
- **Private regime:** presence and protocol both read exactly 0.
- **gem5 8 MB runs, from their `config.ini`:**
  - `OCEAN -n514 -p4`, L2 8388608 bytes;
  - `ordered=false` on all 8 L0↔L1 buffers, `randomization=false`;
  - stopped at 50 M and 150 M, any thread.

## Result

Store-time events per 1,000 instructions, window 50–150 M:

| ocean 4c | gem5 `(SS+MT).L1_GETX` | ChampSim (b) protocol | **R_b** | (a) presence | R_a | v1 LLC-mask | R_v1 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **2 MB** | 0.06961 | 0.08794 | **1.263** | 0.00531 | 0.076 | 0.00315 | 0.045 |
| **8 MB** | 0.06660 | 0.08980 | **1.348** | 0.01353 | 0.203 | 0.03061 | 0.460 |

**Primary criterion, 0.5 ≤ R_b ≤ 2 at each LLC size: met at both.**
ChampSim's protocol-view sharer tracking reproduces gem5's store-time
sharing rate on ocean within 26% at 2 MB and 35% at 8 MB, high in both. The
v1 counter, which read sharers off LLC lines, was 22× low at 2 MB. The
directory removes that limitation.

**Directional prediction, R_b ≤ 1 ≤ R_a: fails at both sizes**, in the
direction the addendum warned of. Presence is far *below* gem5 (R_a 0.08 /
0.20): by the time another core stores to a line, the private caches that
read it have usually evicted it. gem5's sharer lists, like the protocol
view, are sticky across silent clean evictions. So the protocol view is the
gem5-like measure, and presence is not an upper bound.

**Hierarchy check: passes.** Shared-cache demand accesses per KI, ChampSim
over gem5: 0.89 at 2 MB, 0.99 at 8 MB.

**Window check.** gem5's rate over its spans:
- 2 MB: 0.0396 / 0.0696 / 0.0800 (0–50 M / 50–150 M / 150 M–end), a 2.02×
  spread, just over the 2× bound. So the verdict is stated per span: **R_b =
  2.22 / 1.26 / 1.10**. It falls outside the band only against gem5's first
  50 M, the initialization stretch, which is not the window ChampSim
  measures.
- 8 MB: 0.0443 / 0.0666, a 1.50× spread, within bounds (no whole-program run
  at 8 MB). R_b 2.03 / 1.35.

## What this does and does not establish

- **Established:** on ocean at 4 cores, at two LLC sizes, the overlay plus the
  sharer directory reproduces a real MESI protocol's store-time sharing rate
  within the pre-registered factor of two. Traffic matches as well. Neither
  side was tuned after seeing R; the counter, configs and window are as
  pre-registered.
- **Not established:**
  - **Generality.** Ocean shares little (23× less than canneal). The
    heavy-sharing workload that carries the cross-regime result, canneal, is
    not yet compared against gem5.
  - **Barnes** shares even less: ChampSim counts 37 protocol events in 404 M
    instructions at 2 MB. Its comparison, when its gem5 runs land, will rest
    on very few events and will be reported as such.
- **COALESCE and srrip_bias still read the v1 LLC masks** (block.h), which
  this comparison shows lose sharers at small LLCs. They are inputs to the
  post-B3 policy work, not something changed here: the architecture freeze
  holds until the owner declares B3 landed.
