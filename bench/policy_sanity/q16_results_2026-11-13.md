## Diagnosis of the miss, written before the diagnostic run

**Hypothesis H-Q16a.** The count of 60 is wrong for the barrier form, and
amendment 5's own reasoning says so. It reads: "Why 15 copies, not 30: the
wait after the loads chases 16 ring lines through each 8-way L1D set, so
every reader's X_j is left only in its private L2". In the barrier form the
step ends in `pthread_barrier_wait` (`STEP_WAIT` in
`bench/coherence_micro.c`) and no ring is chased. So each of the 15 readers
still holds X_j in its L1D **and** its L2 when thread 15 stores: 2 copies a
reader, 30 a round, 120.

**Test.** Rerun s16 at level 2 in the barrier form only, on the server, with
the same harness, binary, tracer and settings. The one addition: the
per-request event log is kept (`KEEP_EVENTS=1` in `bench/micro_champsim.sh`,
which copies it to the output directory; default unchanged). The I lines
(private copies invalidated) on the X lines are then counted by cache and core
(`bench/q16_events.py`).

**H-Q16a holds** only if all of the following are true:
- the rerun reads `invalidated 120` again, with every other s16 count as
  before;
- its I lines on the X lines split exactly 60 in L1D caches and 60 in L2C
  caches;
- each of cores 0-14 shows 4 in its L1D and 4 in its L2C, and core 15 none;
- every one of them is written at one of the 4 upgrades. Each I line comes
  right before the U line that caused it (`dir_invalidate_line`, `cache.cc`),
  and that U line must be thread 15's L1D or L2C upgrade.

**If it holds:**
- The barrier form's hand count for s16 is corrected to 30 copies a round
  (120), and the timed form's stays 60. The correction rests on amendment 5's
  derivation, which applies only to timed waits. It was made after the 120
  was seen, and is reported that way.
- Q16 is then re-judged on 66 counts with that one correction, nothing else
  changed.
- `bench/q16_judge.py` takes the form into account for this count only, and
  its self-test is extended to cover it.

**If it does not hold:** Q16 stays failed. The event log then says which step
and which core, and the next note follows from it. No 16-core ratio is read
until Q16 holds.

## The diagnostic run: H-Q16a HOLDS; Q16 HOLDS, 66 of 66

`scripts/q16cs_all.sh diag` (owner's word, after the section above was

|---|---|
| `invalidated 120` again, other s16 counts as before | `xline ... P 0 A 0 U 4 ... U_cores 60 ... G5U_cores 60 invalidated 120` |
| I lines on the X lines: 60 in L1D, 60 in L2C | L1D 60, L2C 60, total 120 |

The control's run has no I line on the X lines, and no I line in either run

**So the barrier form's hand count was wrong, not the simulator.** Readers who
are not made to chase a ring keep X_j in both private levels. The count is
corrected as written beforehand: in the barrier form s16 at level 2
invalidates **30 copies a round (120)**; the timed form stays at 15 (60).
**This correction was made after the 120 was seen.** It rests on amendment 5's
own derivation, which assumed the timed form's ring. It is reported that way.

`bench/q16_judge.py` reads the form from the control's log (`SYNC REPLAY: on`

level 1 0, U_cores 60 in both). Self-test passes (20 met; the injected fault
gives 18 / 2).
