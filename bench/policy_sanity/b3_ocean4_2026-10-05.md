## B3, 2 MB: disagreement, by the pre-registered criterion

| per 1,000 instructions, window 50–150 M | ChampSim | gem5 | ratio |
|---|---|---|---|
| **store-time sharing events** (primary) | **0.00315** | **0.0696** | **R = 0.045** |
| shared-cache demand accesses | 21.01 | 23.59 | 0.89 |
| store misses reaching the shared cache | 5.08 (LLC RFO) | 4.73 (L2 `L1_GETX`, all states) | 1.07 |
| upgrades (ChampSim cannot see them) | — | 0.1055 | — |

**R = 0.045 is far outside the 0.5–2 band: ChampSim sees about 22× fewer
store-time sharing events than gem5.** The criterion needs agreement at both
LLC sizes, so the verdict is disagreement whatever the 8 MB half shows.

**The hierarchy check passes.** Shared-cache traffic agrees within 11%, and
the store-miss stream within 7%. The two simulators send the same traffic to
the shared cache; they differ in what that cache knows about who else holds
a line.

**The gap is pre-registered difference #1, non-inclusion, and it is
measured:**
- ChampSim's LLC knows a line's sharers only while it holds the line. At
  2 MB, **0.98% of store misses hit the LLC** (19,945 of 2,040,492). The
  other 99% find no sharer record, whoever holds the line privately; four
  private 512 KB L2s add up to the 2 MB LLC's whole capacity.
- Among the RFOs that do hit, 6.35% find other sharers. Per RFO access that
  is 0.062%, against gem5's 1.47% of store misses finding another holder:
  the same ~24× gap as R.
- gem5's L2 is inclusive: a line any L1 holds stays in L2 with its sharer
  list. 97.5% of gem5's store misses meet a line nobody holds (`NP`); the
  1.47% that others hold are exactly what ChampSim's LLC has lost.
- **8 MB, ChampSim side** (gem5's 8 MB runs still running): RFO LLC hit rate
  11.86%, store-time events 0.0306 per KI, ten times the 2 MB rate. That is
  the direction non-inclusion predicts. Against gem5's 2 MB window rate it
  would be R ≈ 0.44; the 8 MB verdict waits for gem5's own 8 MB window.

**Window check: steady within 2× only just failed.** gem5's primary rate over
its three spans: 0.0396 (0–50 M), 0.0696 (50–150 M), 0.0800 (150 M–end).
Max over min is 2.02, above the pre-registered 2×, so the verdict is stated
per span. It does not change: R is 0.08, 0.045 and 0.039 against the three
spans, far outside the band in each. Thread start-up is not the cause: at
the 50 M stop the four threads stood at 50.0 / 49.5 / 49.6 / 49.7 M, so
ocean's threads start almost together.

### What this means for the tool

- **The overlay's sharing is real at the memory level:** traffic and
  store-miss streams match gem5 closely.
- **Its LLC sharer tracking is not a coherence directory.** It forgets
  sharers when the LLC evicts a line, and at the 2 MB geometry it forgets
  nearly all of them. Anything read from the LLC sharer masks inherits this:
  the coherence counters, and the inputs of COALESCE and srrip_bias (sharer
  count, MESI state).
- **The cross-regime result (A5) does not rest on these masks.** It compares
  cycle counts under the overlay's aliasing, which the traffic match
  supports. The coherence-aware policies' inputs do rest on them.
- **A fix would track sharers independently of LLC residency** (a sharer
  directory or snoop filter). Under the pre-registration that is a new
  version of the tool, with a new pre-registered comparison, not a change
  made after seeing R. Whether it belongs in the ISPASS paper is the
  owner's decision.

## The ocean prediction (`1f327b7`)

8 baselines, private vs shared, Kendall τ:

| geometry | τ | pre-registered |
|---|---|---|
| fixed 2 MB (the pairing the rule chose) | **+0.71**: 24 concordant, 4 discordant | P1 needs ≥ +0.71: **met, at the boundary** |
| scaled, 2 MB per core | **+0.93** | **met** |

**P1 holds.** The four pairs that flip at 2 MB all involve srrip_bias
(−1.79% → +2.65%) or SRRIP/LRU: the coherence-biased policy moves even on a
workload that barely shares. Canneal at 4 cores, under the same comparison,
gave +0.57; ocean, with 23× less sharing, gives +0.71 / +0.93. That is
consistent with sharing driving the reordering, and on its own it is modest
evidence.

**P2 is partly refuted** (each COALESCE variant moves < 2 pp between regimes):

| COALESCE, speedup over LRU, private → shared | fixed 2 MB | scaled |
|---|---|---|
| variant A | +10.99 → +10.25 (−0.7 pp) ✓ | −14.14 → −14.44 (−0.3 pp) ✓ |
| variant B | +2.56 → +6.32 (+3.8 pp) ✗ | −17.18 → −21.12 (−3.9 pp) ✗ |

Variant B moves 3.8–3.9 pp, against canneal's 16.5. It is smaller by four
times, but outside the stated bound.
