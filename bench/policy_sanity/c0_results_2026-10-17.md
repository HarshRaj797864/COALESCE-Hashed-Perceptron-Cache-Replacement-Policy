## What it means

- **On canneal, charging 20 to 100 cycles for each invalidation changes no
  policy's place.** The cost grows with L as expected (about 1% and 2% at
  100 cycles, 4 and 8 cores) and falls on every policy almost alike.
- On ocean, which shares little, the policies sit close together and a
  per-event cost moves them by up to 1.7 points in both directions; one
  adjacent pair swaps. Its LRU timing anomaly is 0.19%.
- This is a **lower bound** on the cost of coherence: ChampSim's core does
  not wait for plain stores (the design's disclosure).
- **Read with `b3_heldout_results_2026-10-17.md`:** C0's waits follow the
  same protocol view that over-counts on radix. On canneal that view agrees
  with gem5 (R_b 1.26 at 4 cores, 1.72 at 8), so these runs are on the
  validated case.
