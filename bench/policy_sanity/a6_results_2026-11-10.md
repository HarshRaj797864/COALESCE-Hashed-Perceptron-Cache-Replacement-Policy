# A6 scored: the LLC-size sweep (2026-11-10)

Design: `a6_size_sweep_design_2026-10-14.md` (pre-registered 2026-10-14,
before any run). Scorer: `scripts/a6_score.py` (`3574cb4`), self-tested on
four made-up sweeps with hand-known verdicts and 9 injected faults, and
committed before it read any A6 row. Rows: `phasea_rows_v29.txt`,
`b3_rows_v20.txt` (pulled 2026-11-10). A6's 116 runs had ended weeks
before; they were not scored until now.

**Binary and traces, as pre-registered:** the frozen `champsim_b3v4_69ebdcce`,
level 2, canneal threads 1..n, 50 M + 100 M a core. These are the
**drifting** traces of before synchronised replay. Step 5 measured how much
replay changes the rankings: tau +0.72 to +1.00 against the same
configurations on drifting traces (`policy_results_2026-11-10.md`).

## Result: H0 at 4 and at 8 cores

| cores | matched pairs M (shared s vs private n*s) | median M | controls C (same factor n, one regime) | reading |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 4 | +0.86, +0.57, +0.43 (all 3 counted) | **+0.57** | -- **+0.21 to +0.64** (6) | **within** |
| 8 | +1.00, +0.29, +0.07 (all 3 counted) | **+0.29** | -- **-0.07 to +0.71** (6) | **within** |

**H0: not distinguishable from a size effect.** The design reads H0 so: "the
reordering seen across regimes is as large as a change of cache size alone
produces, so the evidence does not separate the two. What stands is the
pressure result: at one size, the private view misjudges the gains."

This agrees with A5's withdrawal (`a5_size_control_2026-10-14.md`) and with
step 5's P11 failure on the synchronised traces, which the size control also
matched.

- **Every matched pair counted:** LRU's load+RFO hit rates are within 1.3 to
  2.7 points in all six (the rule is 5).
- **The order loosens as the cache grows, in both kinds of pair.** Matched
  pairs go from +0.86 to +0.43 at 4c and from +1.00 to +0.07 at 8c with s =
  1, 2, 4 MB; the controls fall the same way (shared 4 vs 32 MB at 8c is
  -0.07). Larger caches bunch the policies together, and small changes then
  reorder them.
- **Near ties:** no configuration meets the design's rule (every policy within
  0.5% of LRU). The closest is 8c shared 32 MB: spread 0.52%, LRU third, the
  top five within 0.04%. It sits in one control (shared 4 vs 32, -0.07);
  the design counts such a pair, which can only make H1 harder.

## The checks (all hold)

- **Q0:** hawkeye and srrip_bias, 4c private, at 2 MB and 8 MB, on the frozen
  binary equal the older rows (`phaseA`, `phaseA_scaled_private`) in
  max_cycles, min_cycles and instructions. LRU at 2 MB: `phaseA_l2_fixed`
  equals `phaseA` at 4c and 8c. So the older private rows stand in.
- **Q1**, on every row used (176: 22 configurations x 8 policies): complete; no trace
  wrap; page pool ok; at least 100 M instructions a core; private 0
  invalidations and 0 aliased fills; shared both non-zero; B3 rows (the
  b3v4 binary's) level 2, self-check 0, stale-holders 0, 3n cores
  registered; no two policies share max_cycles in a configuration.
- Sources (the design's table): A6's own trees at 1, 4, 16 MB (4c) and 1, 4,
  8, 32 MB (8c); 2 MB from `phaseA_l2_fixed` (shared) and `phaseA` (private);
  2 MB a core from `phaseA_l2_scaled` (shared) and `phaseA_scaled_private`
  (private), LRU private from the `phaseA_l2_*` trees.
