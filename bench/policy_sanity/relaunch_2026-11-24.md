## 3. radix16's g6 reference: restarted alone

- **Command:** `scripts/relaunch_all.sh radix16`.
- **How it runs:** through the trace runner's own `job()` (`VARIANT=pb
  ~/sync_server_pb.sh __job radix16 radix 16 0 50000000 100000000
  radix_16`), detached. That is the same file that ran it first, with:
  - its JOBS line, checked equal to `~/partB/radix_16/JOBS`;
  - the binary in `~/sync_pb/BIN` (`champsim_g6_1e3060c1`);
  - the same config, window and traces.
- **Not used:** the pb chain. Its guard wants 60 GB free, and it would also
  revisit every other key.
- **The dead run's log** is copied to `run.log.dead_<time>` and not read.
- **Refused if:**
  - the pb chain is alive;
  - the reference is complete;
  - a radix16 reference process runs;
  - the JOBS line differs;
  - the traces have no DONE.
- **Checks after the start:** the new log shows its window lines, and a
  process is running.

## 4. Part B on F-C, amendment 13 of `breadth_bc_design`: radix16 may start before its reference completes

- **Why:**
  - At the rate seen under load (about 9 days to 130 M), the reference
    followed by its rerun could miss amendment 11's cut-off of Sat 5 Dec
    23:59.
  - The rerun needs only the reference's inputs (config, window, traces),
    not its result.
- **The rule:**
  - A rerun named in `EARLY` (default `radix16`) may start once its
    reference's log shows `Warmup Instructions:` and `Simulation
    Instructions:`.
  - The window comes from those lines.
  - The traces come from the key's sizing record (`~/partB/<key>/PROGS`:
    field 6 the traced threads, field 8 thread 0's cut), as the trace
    runner's `traces_for` lists them.
- **QE-early, in the runner's guards:** the rule must give exactly the trace
  list (the "CPU n runs" lines) of every completed 8- and 16-thread
  reference, at least 10 of them. Each EARLY rerun's traces must exist, one
  a core.
  - **Checked on 2026-11-23, read-only**, on the server's own sizing records
    and logs: **27 of 27 equal.**
  - Two injected faults were each caught: a wrong thread-0 rule on
    barnes_8, and a missing radix_16 trace.
- **Unchanged:** at scoring, `fcrows` (lock_fix_score.qualify) still
  compares each rerun's window, CPU count and traces with its completed
  reference. An early rerun whose inputs differed would FAIL there, and a
  reference that never completes leaves the rerun PENDING. Amendment 11's
  cut-off and every other rule are unchanged.
- **Order on the day:** the reference is restarted first (§ 3), and partBfc
  only after its log shows the window. The early rerun's only race is a
  freshly truncated log.
